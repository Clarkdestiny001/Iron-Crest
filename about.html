About Us

Our Story

IronCrest started as a small family business in New York and now builds projects across North America, Europe and the Middle East.

Our Story

Building Since 1998

IronCrest Construction was founded in 1998 by Robert Titan, a civil engineer.

What began as a small residential construction firm in New York now works across North America, Europe and the Middle East. In 28 years we have completed more than 350 projects worth over $12 billion.

We give every project, large or small, the same attention. That approach has brought us repeat work from corporations, government agencies and private clients.

Safety First

Our Safety Commitment

At IronCrest Construction, safety comes first on every site. We have had zero lost-time incidents across our last 50 projects.

Our safety program includes daily toolbox talks, ongoing training, protective equipment and regular site inspections. Every team member can stop work if they see a safety concern.

We hold OSHA VPP Star certification and have received several national safety awards.

Green Building

Sustainability at Our Core

More than 40 of our projects are LEED certified, and we work to reduce the environmental impact of every build.

We use recycled and locally sourced materials, energy-efficient systems and careful planning to reduce waste, and we add renewable energy where the project allows.

We have set a goal of carbon-neutral operations by 2030, and we are investing in green technology, electric equipment and carbon offset programs.
