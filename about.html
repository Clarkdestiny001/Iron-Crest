Our Story

Building Legacies Since 1998

IronCrest Construction was founded in 1998 by Robert Titan, a civil engineer with a vision to transform the construction industry through innovation and uncompromising quality standards.

What began as a small residential construction firm in New York has grown into a global construction powerhouse with operations across North America, Europe, and the Middle East. Over the past 28 years, we have completed over 350 projects worth more than $12 billion.

Our success is built on a simple philosophy: every project, regardless of size, deserves the same level of dedication, expertise, and attention to detail. This commitment has earned us the trust of Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and discerning private clients worldwide.

Safety First

Our Safety Commitment

At IronCrest Construction, safety is not just a priority—it's a core value embedded in every aspect of our operations. We maintain an industry-leading safety record with zero lost-time incidents across our last 50 projects.

Our comprehensive safety program includes daily toolbox talks, continuous training, state-of-the-art personal protective equipment, and rigorous site inspections. Every team member is empowered to stop work if they identify any safety concern.

We are proud to hold OSHA VPP Star certification and have been recognized with multiple national safety awards for our commitment to protecting our people and partners.

Green Building

Sustainability at Our Core

We are committed to building a sustainable future. With over 40 LEED-certified projects and counting, IronCrest Construction leads the industry in environmentally responsible construction practices.

Our sustainability initiatives include using recycled and locally sourced materials, implementing energy-efficient systems, minimizing construction waste through advanced planning, and integrating renewable energy solutions into our projects.

We have pledged to achieve carbon-neutral operations by 2030 and are actively investing in green technologies, electric construction equipment, and carbon offset programs to make this vision a reality.
