Frequently Asked Questions

Project timelines vary significantly based on scope, complexity, and scale. A residential renovation may take 3-6 months, while a commercial high-rise can take 18-36 months. During our initial consultation, we provide a detailed timeline tailored to your specific project.

We provide transparent, detailed cost estimates based on architectural plans, material selections, labor requirements, and project timeline. Our estimates include a comprehensive breakdown of all costs with no hidden fees. We also offer value engineering options to help optimize your budget.

Yes, we manage the entire permitting process including zoning approvals, building permits, environmental clearances, and inspections. Our team has extensive experience navigating regulatory requirements across all jurisdictions where we operate.

Safety is our top priority. We implement comprehensive safety protocols including daily toolbox talks, continuous training, PPE requirements, site inspections, and emergency response plans. We hold OSHA VPP Star certification and maintain a zero lost-time incident record.

We provide a comprehensive 2-year workmanship warranty on all projects, with extended warranties available for structural elements. We also offer maintenance programs to ensure your building continues to perform at its best for years to come.
